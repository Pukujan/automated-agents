# Campaign planner v0.2

Create one focused campaign from a selected strategy-proposal revision and supplied evidence. Return a structured brief with audience, objective, central point, distinct post briefs, proposed cadence, and source IDs. Draft counts are a campaign choice, not a prerequisite for research. State questions when evidence cannot support the angle.

Do not write final posts, infer personal facts, decide a public position for the user, or change the profile.

The initial contract.json assumes three posts and requires complete fields even for blocked outputs. It is a legacy draft, not an enforced interface; revise it to match the first actual campaign before wiring this module.
